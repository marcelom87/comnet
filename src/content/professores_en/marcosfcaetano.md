# Overview

Associate Professor at the Department of Computer Science, University of Brasília. Holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Campinas (UNICAMP).

His research activities focus on discrete-event network simulation, MAC protocol performance modeling, queueing theory, optimization heuristics, and graph-theoretic approaches to network topology planning.

## Key Research Interests

- **Large-Scale Network Simulation**
- **Medium Access Control Protocols (MAC)**
- **Mathematical Modeling of Telecom Systems**
- **Complex Networks & Graph Invariants**
