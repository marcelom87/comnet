# Overview

Holds a Ph.D. from the University of Leeds, England (1996), M.Sc. in Electronics and Computer Engineering from the Aeronautics Institute of Technology - ITA (1989), and B.S. in Computer Science from UFRGS (1985). She is a Full Professor at the University of Brasília (UnB).

Her research focuses on Artificial Intelligence, multi-agent systems, ontology engineering, distributed knowledge bases, and autonomous intelligent agents applied to complex networks and institutional processes.

## Key Research Interests

- **Artificial Intelligence & Multi-Agent Systems**
- **Knowledge-Based Systems & Semantic Web**
- **Machine Learning & Big Data Mining**
- **Autonomous Decision Support**
