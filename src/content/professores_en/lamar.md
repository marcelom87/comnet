# Overview

Holds a B.S. (1992) and M.Sc. (1995) in Electrical Engineering from UFRGS, and a Ph.D. in Information Engineering from Nagoya University, Japan (2001). He is an Associate Professor at the University of Brasília.

His research focuses on neural network architectures, signal processing algorithms, pattern recognition, and machine learning models applied to digital communications and multimodal data analysis.

## Key Research Interests

- **Digital Signal Processing (DSP)**
- **Artificial Neural Networks & Deep Learning**
- **Pattern Recognition & Computer Vision**
- **Digital Communications & Signal Analysis**
