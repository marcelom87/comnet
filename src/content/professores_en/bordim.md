# Overview

Holds a B.S. in Computer Science from UPF (1994), M.Sc. in Computer Science from UFRGS (1997), and Ph.D. in Information Engineering from Hiroshima University, Japan (2001). He is an Associate Professor at CIC/UnB.

His research explores algorithmic foundations of parallel and distributed systems, energy-efficient protocols for sensor networks, and specialized embedded computing architectures.

## Key Research Interests

- **Parallel & Distributed Algorithms**
- **Wireless Sensor Networks & Ad-Hoc Systems**
- **Computer Architecture & Hardware-Software Co-Design**
- **Embedded Systems for IoT**
