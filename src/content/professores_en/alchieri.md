# Overview

Holds a B.S. in Computer Science from UFSC (2004), M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from UFSC (2006), and Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Lisbon, Portugal (2011). He is currently an Associate Professor at the Department of Computer Science (CIC/UnB).

His research interests encompass dependable and fault-tolerant computing, Byzantine agreement protocols, distributed consensus algorithms, blockchain networks, and cybersecurity in cloud architectures.

## Key Research Interests

- **Dependable Distributed Systems & Fault Tolerance**
- **Distributed Consensus & Blockchain Architectures**
- **Cloud & Edge Computing Security**
- **High-Performance Parallel Algorithms**
