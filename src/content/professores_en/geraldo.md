# Overview

Holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Computational Mathematics from the University of São Paulo (USP), including a research stay at the University of Ottawa (Canada). He is a researcher at COMNET and Professor at UnB.

His investigations focus on smart urban environments, wireless sensor network optimization, vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communications, and low-latency edge computing topologies.

## Key Research Interests

- **Smart Cities & Urban Computing**
- **Internet of Things (IoT) & Sensing Protocols**
- **Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs)**
- **Edge Computing & Fog Infrastructure**
