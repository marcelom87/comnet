# Overview

Assistant Professor at the Department of Computer Science (CIC), University of Brasília (UnB). He completed his Ph.D. in Computer Science at Lancaster University, United Kingdom (2018).

His research investigates self-adaptive software architectures, distributed middleware, decentralized computing, and communication protocols for harsh or intermittent network connectivity environments.

## Key Research Interests

- **Distributed Systems & Cloud/Edge Infrastructure**
- **Delay-Tolerant Networks (DTN)**
- **Cyber-Physical Systems & IoT Middleware**
- **Self-Adaptive Software Systems**
