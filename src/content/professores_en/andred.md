# Overview

Holds a B.S. in Computer Engineering from PUC-Campinas (2002), M.Sc. (2005), and Ph.D. (2009) in Computer Science from the University of Campinas (UNICAMP). He is currently an Associate Professor at the Department of Computer Science, University of Brasília (UnB). 

His primary research interests include optical networks, elastic optical architectures, traffic grooming, queueing theory, and network slicing orchestration in future telecommunications infrastructure.

## Key Research Interests

- **Optical & Elastic Networks (EON)**
- **Traffic Engineering & Routing Optimization**
- **Performance Modeling & Network Simulation**
- **Network Virtualization & Slicing**
