# Overview

Holds a B.S. in Systems Engineering from Universidad Francisco Marroquín, Guatemala, M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from UnB (1997), and Ph.D. in Computer Science from UFRGS (2004). She is an Associate Professor at CIC/UnB.

Her research covers QoS/QoE provisioning models, traffic classification, network security monitoring, mobile ad-hoc protocols, and telecom decision management architectures.

## Key Research Interests

- **Quality of Service (QoS) & Quality of Experience (QoE)**
- **Network Security & Anomaly Detection**
- **Mobile Routing Protocols & Mesh Networks**
- **Decision Support Systems in Telecommunications**
