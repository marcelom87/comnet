# Overview

Associate Professor at the Department of Computer Science, University of Brasília (UnB). Previously served as R&D Coordinator at CTIC / RNP (Brazilian National Research and Education Network). He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from UFRGS (2019) with a doctoral research stay at the CONNECT Centre, Trinity College Dublin, Ireland (2016).

His research focuses on Cloud Radio Access Networks (C-RAN), Open RAN (O-RAN), Intent-Based Networking (IBN), Software-Defined Radio (SDR), cognitive wireless communications, and zero-touch autonomous management of next-generation 6G telecom networks.

## Key Research Interests

- **Wireless & Mobile Networks (5G/6G)**
- **Intent-Based Networks (IBN)**
- **Cloud Radio Access Networks (C-RAN)**
