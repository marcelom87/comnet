## Resumo do Projeto

The main objective of the project is to define and develop a Web-friendly social network tool to allow integration of the academic community of University of Brasília (UnB). The project uses semantic resources, we have extended the ontology CONSCIENTIAS of CNPq, we have defined a reputation model to implement in the social network, and uses geo-referencing technique for easy finding the researches in the three different UnB Campus. This project started in 2011 and is funded by the Dean of Research and Postgraduate Studies of UnB..
