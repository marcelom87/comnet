## Resumo do Projeto

SCI-synergy is an online artifact built upon the Design-Science Research paradigm to provide intra and inter-institutional scientific collaboration network views. The Digital Bibliography Library Project repository (DBLP) is being used as data source with a graph NoSQL database (neo4j) as storage and a python web app to present the information. SCI-Synergy was validated using data from researchers of five Computer Science graduate programs of Federal Brazilian Universities: Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG), University of Sao Paulo (USP), Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte (UFRN), Federal University of Amazonas (UFAM) and University of Brasilia (UnB). SCI-Synergy presents views of the collaboration networks between researchers considering researchers not linked to the cited programs, but with co- authorship relationships..
