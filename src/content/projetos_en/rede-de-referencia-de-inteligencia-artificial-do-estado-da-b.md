## Project Overview

The BAH.IA Project aims to structure actions to disseminate and strengthen skills in Artificial Intelligence (AI) Enabling Technology in the State of Bahia. To this end, it envisages the creation and operationalization of the Artificial Intelligence Reference Network of the State of Bahia (ESPAÇO BAH.IA), integrating researchers and AI specialists within partner ICTs. The project also seeks to establish connections between the ESPAÇO BAH.IA Network and other regional and national networks and projects, in addition to producing training materials and guidelines for AI training aimed at different target audiences. Another goal of the project is to develop a functional prototype of an online platform for training and capacity building in the area. Furthermore, the project aims to map technological demands, identifying opportunities for research, development and innovation projects using AI. In the long term, these initiatives are expected to boost the development of AI-based solutions in the State of Bahia, fostering technological advancement, innovation and qualification of professionals and students in the area.
