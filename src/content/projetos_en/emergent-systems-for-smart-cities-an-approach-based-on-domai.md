## Project Overview

The overall aim of the project is to propose and evaluate a new methodology and associated tools for the development of software for smart cities, including both middleware and applications. The project will leverage on the proponents? existing research on emergent and adaptive systems, software platforms for smart cities, and model-driven domain- specific middleware. The ability to automatically assemble dynamically adaptive systems from the specification of component dependencies and test cases, as provided by the Dana language developed at Lancaster University, will form the basis of the programming model promoted by the new methodology. An existing software services platform for smart cities called InterSCity, currently under development in Brazil (in a collaboration that involves UFG), will provide the underlying support services for the programming model, including the scalable integration of heterogeneous data sources, resource discovery, and data collection. The platform is built as an extensible set of microservices. Furthermore, it promotes the development of a marketplace of reusable microservices that serve as components for building applications. In this project, we aim to develop the necessary support for composing applications out of such reusable microservices based on the principles and tools of emergent systems. Within the context of the above research aims, we also plan to investigate the contribution that the model-driven domain- specific middleware approach can provide, both as a high-level methodology for specifying applications (that can be built as emergent adaptive systems) and as a middleware substrate that supports application modeling constructs tailored to specific domains and with precise operational semantics...
