## Project Overview

This work aims to propose the development of a vehicle monitoring system in urban traffic using computer vision. This research and development project is being proposed by the Digital Video Processing Laboratory (LPVD - UFPR) and submitted to the Human Resources in Transport Training Center (CEFTRU - UnB), as the development of a tool that solves local needs, as well as the possibility of registering a national patent.
