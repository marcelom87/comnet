## Project Overview

Traditional recommendation techniques do not consider user preferences according to context. However, recommendation systems that add contextual elements are generally based on syntactic description or numerical values ​​explicitly entered by the user, which can generate recommendations outside the desired domain and limit the system's transparency in the user-environment interaction, one of the objectives of Pervasive Computing. For the recommendation process to be personalized and transparent to the user, an application is needed that proactively filters the content according to the user's current situation and preferences. In this sense, the objective of this work is to present an architecture based on agents that allows the incremental extraction of semantically related behavioral patterns and offers contextualized information to users on a university campus, helping them and facilitating access to academic and cultural information.
