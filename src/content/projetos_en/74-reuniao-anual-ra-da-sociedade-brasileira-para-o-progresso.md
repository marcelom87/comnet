## Project Overview

Project by the University of Brasília (UnB) to promote the 74th Annual Meeting of the SBPC, to be held between the 24th and 30th of July 2022. The meeting will take place in a hybrid format, with in-person and virtual activities, on the four UnB campuses (Brasília, Ceilândia, Gama and Planaltina). The central theme of this edition is Science, Independence and National Sovereignty with activities from different areas of knowledge.
