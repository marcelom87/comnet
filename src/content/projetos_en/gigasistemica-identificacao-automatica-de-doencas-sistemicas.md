## Project Overview

This project's general objective is to develop machine learning algorithms, including convolutional neural networks (RNC), with the purpose of establishing the early diagnosis of chronic systemic diseases that have a high prevalence and high morbidity, or predicting the appearance of these diseases, based on dental images. For this purpose, images from dental panoramic radiographs (RP) and cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) will be used as input data for these algorithms to early diagnose or even predict systemic diseases, such as cardiovascular diseases, type 2 diabetes and osteoporosis. Likewise, the use of these tools could, in the near future, predict chronic systemic diseases, through the automated identification of patterns not perceptible to the human eye. To investigate this possibility, a project was created in a partnership between the University of Brasília, the Dentistry area, the Computing area and the Image Processing area with Redecomep GigaCandanga, which constitutes a metropolitan education and research network. The project team consists of specialists in the various areas of knowledge involved, as well as the basic infrastructure necessary to implement the project.
