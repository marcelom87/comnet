## Project Overview

The present research project involves research in the areas of Bioinformatics, Comparative Genomics and High Performance Computing, with the main objective of proposing and evaluating efficient parallel algorithms for executing Bioinformatics applications on Heterogeneous High Performance Platforms, composed of clusters of multicores and accelerators (GPUs, FPGAs and/or CellBEs)...
