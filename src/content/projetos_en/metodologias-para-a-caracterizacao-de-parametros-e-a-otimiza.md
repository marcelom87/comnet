## Project Overview

The general objective of this research project is to systematize a set of actions for the development of a laboratory environment for modeling multimedia traffic and transmission parameters in wireless networks, with the purpose of efficiently supporting actions in the area of ​​network planning, particularly in the tasks of sizing network elements, admission control, calculating QoS metrics and traffic policing.
