## Project Overview

The SEMFOGO-DF project aims to develop a solution within the context of Smart Cities, by applying Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Computer Vision (VC) algorithms to identify fires, with the aim of providing technological support to the Military Fire Department of the Federal District (CBM-DF) in the rapid treatment of emergencies, based on reliable and intelligent analysis of information collected in a distributed and automated way in the Brasília-DF area. The special focus of the proposed solution is fire incidents in the metropolitan and cerrado region of Brasília. The project proposes the implementation of a framework that will allow the capture of video images and their processing in real time to identify accidents and trigger alarms when detected. The images will be obtained through cameras installed at strategic points in the city. In this project, the proposal is that the first point of installation of the cameras is the digital TV tower. The images will be transmitted through Redecomep's high-speed optical connectivity infrastructure managed by Gigacandanga.
