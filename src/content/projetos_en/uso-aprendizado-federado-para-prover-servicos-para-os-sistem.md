## Project Overview

Predicting urban mobility, monitoring traffic dynamics in cities, limiting the resources involved, effectively collecting heterogeneous information from urban areas and effectively using communication infrastructure resources are essential elements for Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS). The provision of smart services in urban spaces provided by ITS directly depends on correctly addressing these and other challenges. In particular, solutions involving the operationalization of machine learning models may require broad and consistent data collection, which may raise questions related to user privacy. The federated learning paradigm (FL- Federated Learning) has been adopted in this context, with the aim of ensuring adequate data processing while allowing the use of distributed computing power and with less dependence on stable connectivity. The proposed project is based on the hypothesis that an ITS can provide, characterize and model urban space-aware services using urban environment data and deep FL models. Therefore, the objective of this research is to investigate how elements extracted from the urban environment through data impact traffic management in large metropolises, proposing new services that are aware of urban environments for ITS. Funding line: Call CNPq/MCTI/FNDCT N 22/2024 - Knowledge Brazil - Support for Network Projects with Brazilian Researchers Abroad.
