## Project Overview

This project involves research and development of new multicast traffic routing functionalities on Digitel S/A routers. Initially, the need to extend support for the PIM (Protocol-Independent Multicast) protocol in IPv4 to the following modes is defined: 1. Denso, also called PIM-DM; 2. Source Specific Multicast Mode, or PIM-SSM..
