## Project Overview

This project proposes the development of protocols to solve fundamental problems found in the design of distributed applications. These solutions will be aimed at dynamic environments, where the composition and topology of the system can undergo arbitrary changes. Furthermore, such solutions must provide the attributes of reliability, availability and integrity for the applications that use them. This project was developed during the sandwich doctorate internship, by doctoral student Eduardo Alchieri, at the University of Lisbon - Portugal, together with the Navigators research group.
