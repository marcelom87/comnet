## Project Overview

This research, development and innovation project aims to carry out a study of technological innovations, in the areas of information technology, with an emphasis on systems interoperability, corporate management, management processes, network management and data management, for strategic areas of the General Coordination of Information Technology (CGTI) of the Attorney General's Office of the National Treasury.
