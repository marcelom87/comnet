## Project Overview

Contemporary systems are often deployed in highly volatile, heterogeneous and therefore quite complex operational environments. Smart Cities are a notable example that illustrates all aspects of the complexity of modern systems. In creating the software that makes up Smart Cities, technologies are used that aim to mitigate problems in the creation and management of modern systems, such as the use of micro-services to build the platform that abstracts the infrastructure of devices spread across the city, and Software Defined Networks (SDN) for the Internet of Things (IoT) to provide greater flexibility in the interconnection of devices. However, these technologies, despite being used to adapt the software at runtime, still require the definition of the system's adaptation logic that determines when and to which configuration the software will adapt. A promising approach that enables systems in any application domain to learn, at runtime, their adaptation logic is called Emerging Software Systems (ESS). The present proposal aims to explore the concept of ESS, together with micro-services and SDN for IoT, in the autonomic composition of Smart Cities software components, dealing with the high dynamicity of the operational environments present in this application domain. Finally, the aim is to contribute to leveraging the state of the art of adaptive and autonomic systems and their role in managing the complexity present in the Smart Cities software ecosystem.
