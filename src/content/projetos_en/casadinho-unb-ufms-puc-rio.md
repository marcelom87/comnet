## Project Overview

The **Casadinho Project (CAPES/CNPq)** was a major inter-institutional scientific cooperation program established to promote the advancement and consolidation of computer science research groups and graduate programs across the Midwest and Southeast regions of Brazil.

The initiative united research teams from the University of Brasília (UnB), Federal University of Mato Grosso do Sul (UFMS), Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio), University of São Paulo (USP), and State University of Campinas (UNICAMP).

Within COMNET at CIC/UnB, the project supported faculty and student academic exchanges, joint research missions, cooperative thesis advising, and high-impact publications in networking protocols, distributed systems, fault tolerance, and intelligent multi-agent systems.
