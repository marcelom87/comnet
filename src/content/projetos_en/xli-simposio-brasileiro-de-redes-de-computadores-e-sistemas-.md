## Project Overview

The Brazilian Symposium on Computer Networks and Distributed Systems (SBRC) is an annual event held by the Brazilian Computing Society (SBC) and the National Computer Network Laboratory (LARC). Over more than four decades of existence, the SBRC has become the most important national scientific event on computer networks and distributed systems, and one of the most attended in the area of ​​computer science. SBRC's excellent reputation can be seen in the quality and significant number of work submissions. Its main track has received an average of 300 submissions annually, of which between 25 and 30 are accepted for publication and oral presentation at the symposium. Other characteristics such as the diversity and scope of activities carried out, the current nature of the topics covered and the professionalism of its organization contributed to the SBRC being consolidated in the calendar of national scientific events and is the most important event in the area in South America.
