## Project Overview

The complexity of processes and activities in public spheres, regardless of their purpose and target audience, increasingly raises the issue of the executing body's control capacity with the ultimate objective, and demands resources that go beyond human management capacity. Therefore, many processes lack the intervention of automatisms that computer systems offer. Among the various options and lines of development for these systems to be employable in the public sphere, and also, due to the fine line in which complexity and current bureaucracy, these allow deviations in their purpose (e.g.: fraud in bidding and services provided) to occur with a certain frequency, inside and outside these user bodies. In this line, the use of computational techniques aimed at anti-fraud protocols become fundamental parts throughout the process chain. Therefore, the aforementioned actions, inherent in a large part of the public service process, make it difficult for the public body to carry out the necessary inspection, and make monitoring all activities ultimately carried out by the body, and their return to society, a task that requires enormous resources. In this context, with the main focus of employing computer systems to automate activities and processes in the public sphere, as well as implementing techniques to combat fraud, the idea of ​​employing and innovative combination of authentication and user identification techniques using biometrics was developed in this work plan, using as a reference the structure of distributed systems so that, together with trust protocols, contributions can be made both in the area of ​​combating fraud and in the improvement and development of innovative proposals and techniques in the areas of biometrics and distributed systems. The issue of biometric identification and authentication already has consolidated solutions globally, and a wide range of techniques and methodologies can be applied for different purposes. Along these lines, scientific contributions in various fields can be proposed, such as fingerprints, facial recognition, writer recognition and even more daring methods, such as user recognition based on brain behavior (known as brain prints). Furthermore, improvements and advances in these systems are possible when used on various computing platforms, including mobile ones. Due to the way process management systems work, in addition to the issue of security and combating fraud, it is necessary to implement policies that incorporate inherent characteristics of complex systems and of a practically ubiquitous nature, such as those presented in distributed systems. The growing need to incorporate a computational ecosystem with a high diversity of architectures and devices (cloud, desktops, mobile devices in general), and with demands outside the traditional and limited client-server model, the interaction between them requires a high commitment, not only in computational load, but also in the organization and management of their activities, which only the use of distributed systems techniques can be achieved. Therefore, a growing demand for new methodologies specifically in this area of ​​distributed systems to adapt and meet new challenges and innovations in the development of complex systems, should and tends to be the target of intense research and innovation work in this field. I.a Description of the Problem In view of the issue exposed above, as well as the need to incorporate all methodologies and techniques, from different areas, and for different purposes than expected, this research project proposes the development of new techniques and methodologies that allow the fusion of attributes and aspects of biometric systems and distributed systems.
