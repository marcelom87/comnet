## Project Overview

The project "Students? Present!" represents a partnership between students from the Department of Computer Science and the Deanships of Community Affairs and Extension aimed at developing prototypes of applications of social interest for use by the academic community on the University of Brasília campuses. Each academic semester, a prototype of an application of interest to the academic community will be developed. The application domains of these applications are of interest to these Deanates.
