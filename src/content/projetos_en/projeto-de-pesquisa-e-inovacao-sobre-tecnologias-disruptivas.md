## Project Overview

The research and innovation project's general objective is to identify analyzes in the technological, economic, regulatory theory and human capital development areas related to the innovative and disruptive technology called Open RAN. The specific objectives of research and innovation are aimed at elucidating technological advances and identifying possible impacts on the economy and the need to adapt the regulatory model of the National Telecommunications Agency related to the implementation in Brazil of Open RAN and open software technologies that are disaggregated from hardware. This involves research and innovation to identify and propose technical, economic and regulatory analyzes regarding Open RAN.
