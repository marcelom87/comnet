## Project Overview

This project aims to study and develop techniques for creating graphics accelerator circuits. The implementation in FPGA (Field Programmable Gate Array) is due to the low cost and versatility of this implementation. Because, even using a low-cost, low-speed FPGA chip, acceleration techniques can be developed, tested and proposed. The final performance will depend solely on the available hardware, and the accelerator can be implemented on high-cost and high-speed FPGAs without significant modifications to the proposed design. Graphics accelerators can be understood as GPUs (Graphical Processing Units) or just circuits that assist the main processor (CPU) in tasks of projecting 3D space onto the 2D screen, rendering images, ray tracing, sprite processing, among many others. Nowadays, the main engine driving the advancement of chip technology is undoubtedly the immense gaming market. Increasingly complex and more realistic games require high-performance graphics processing, both in terms of speed and in terms of energy consumption (thermal dissipation).
