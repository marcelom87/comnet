## Project Overview

Simulation models present themselves as a promising field of multidisciplinary research. A special class of simulation models is represented by spatial or landscape models, which simulate changes in environmental attributes across geographic territory. The use of these models aims to help understand the causal mechanisms and development processes of environmental systems, being able to determine how they evolve in the face of a set of factors. This research project proposal presents the definition and development of a simulation tool using intelligent techniques to aid Environmental Management. During the research, it is planned to define simulation models such as deforestation in the Amazon and the Cerrado biome. Through the prototype of the functional tool and the defined simulation models, it will be possible to carry out spatial simulations that serve as an instrument to support territorial planning decisions, validating the proposal not only as an object of academic research, but as a real resource within the scope of public institutions linked to the Federal District.
