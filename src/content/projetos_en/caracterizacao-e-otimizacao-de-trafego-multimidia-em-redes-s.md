## Project Overview

This project aims to promote the development of a wireless network laboratory infrastructure in the Department of Computing at the University of Brasília that allows the development of research in the area in the Central-West region, as well as studying and developing methodologies for the planning and optimization process in multimedia networks and formulating techniques to provide adequate QoS differentiation between different classes of traffic.
