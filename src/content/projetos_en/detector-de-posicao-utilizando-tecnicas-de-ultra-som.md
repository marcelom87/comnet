BIBLIOGRAPHICAL RESEARCH STUDY OF EQUATIONS AND FACTORS THAT INFLUENCE THE ACCURACY OF MEASUREMENTS DESIGN AND SIMULATION DOC CIRCUITS FOR TWO-DIMENSIONAL DETERMINATION; PRECISION MEASUREMENT OF THE PROTOTYPE; DEVELOPMENT OF THE THREE-DIMENSIONAL SYSTEM; DEVELOPMENT OF THE DETERMINATION SYSTEM BODY ORIENTATION (POINT); DEVELOPMENT OF APPLICATION AND EDUCATIONAL SOFTWARE..
