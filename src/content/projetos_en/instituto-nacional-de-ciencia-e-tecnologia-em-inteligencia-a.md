## Project Overview

Creation of the National Institute of Science and Technology in Artificial Intelligence (INCT), which was called IAIA. The project aims to bring together and stimulate collaborative scientific, technological and innovation research in Artificial Intelligence (AI), becoming a reference for Brazilian AI, internationally recognized for its research quality. The initiative will develop a strong training program for human resources training in AI, including applied AI. The IAIA has 87 researchers linked to 23 universities in the country. The financing was provided through Notice No. 58/2022 - National Institutes of Science and Technology Program - INCT of CNPq.
