## Project Overview

With the widespread adoption of mobile devices and wireless campus networks, delivering personalized electronic services (e-Services) in academic institutions became a central challenge for enhancing user interaction and administrative efficiency.

The **UnB e-Services (OSE)** project investigated context-aware service delivery by modeling spatial and temporal attributes to dynamically serve community members according to their current location and preferences across the campus.

The project designed and developed a software framework grounded in intelligent multi-agent systems (MAS) to autonomously orchestrate and deliver context-sensitive electronic services across the Darcy Ribeiro University Campus at UnB.
