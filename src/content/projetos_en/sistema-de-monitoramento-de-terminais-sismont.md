## Project Overview

The National Land Transport Agency? ANTT, through an agreement signed with the Human Resources in Transport Training Center at the University of Brasília? CEFTRU/UnB, has been seeking innovations and improvements in monitoring, inspection activities and compliance with the granting conditions for the provision of Interstate and International Road Passenger Transport services, so that it operates satisfying the conditions of regularity, efficiency, safety, timeliness, generality, courtesy in the provision of services and reasonable fares. In addition to the monitoring activities of Interstate and International Passenger Transport in the terminals, carried out by teams from CEFTRU/UnB, agreement 003/ANTT/2006 aims, as one of its products (Stage V), to develop a systemic methodology aimed at automating and reducing the costs of online monitoring data collection. This report presents a study of currently available technologies that can assist in the process of monitoring the interstate bus passenger transport system. The technologies studied were distributed in different scenarios, which were proposed seeking to aggregate collected data and the increase in technology, with the concern that in the evolution of the scenarios there would be no loss of applied technologies. The results of the analyzes carried out here, combined with ANTT's need for information, form the basis for preparing the implementation plan for the automation process.
