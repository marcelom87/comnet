## Project Overview

The project in the area of ​​Bioinformatics aims to investigate whether the search for homology can be solved by machine learning algorithms or whether the structure of the available data and the nature of the problem (search for subsequences) limit this approach. These methods address challenging biological applications such as identifying telomerase RNAs (TERs) in fungi and riboswitches. The proposal integrates four Brazilian universities (UnB, UNICAMP, USP São Carlos and UFMS), integrated with the German University of Leipzig. The team has been collaborating on research in the area of ​​bioinformatics since 2014.
