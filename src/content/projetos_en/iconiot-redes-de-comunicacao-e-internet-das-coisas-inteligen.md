## Project Overview

The consolidation of the Internet into a global and pervasive communication network led to the emergence of a global Society connected and generating a massive amount of data. The Internet is, in reality, an interconnection of heterogeneous networks, flexible enough to allow the deployment of new applications. On the other hand, the dissemination of sensors in the most varied devices of everyday use allows the construction of intelligent systems, such as smart cities, which have the potential to provide greater well-being for Society, as well as boost the Economy. The emergence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) as a fundamental area for solving difficult problems has led to its adoption in different systems, such as the Internet of Things (IoT) and communication networks. The processing of raw data generated by sensors has the potential to generate information, knowledge and added value. The multiplicity and diversity of information in the operation of communications networks demands possible solutions derived only through the use of AI. This proposal aims to explore the introduction of intelligence in future communications and IoT networks, so that there are networks that enable new services capable of responding to the complex demands of Society. The aim is to adopt the methodology of developing intelligent technologies inherent to the functioning of these networks and their services. Furthermore, the generation of specialized critical mass will enable the Brazilian State to remain at the forefront of the new digital era and its great opportunities for generating wealth and well-being. The project fundamentally involves strategic enabling and production areas, such as the Internet of Things, Artificial Intelligence and Communications, aimed at producing solutions for strategic areas, namely: production, industry and services; quality of life; health; sustainable development, environmental preservation and smart cities.
