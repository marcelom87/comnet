## Project Overview

CNPq Project - Universal Notice - Track B - Number 481903/2009-7: This project's main objective is to study the main problems encountered in the development of applications that have safe and reliable progress, in dynamic distributed systems. Therefore, in this project, studies of concepts, models and problems involving dynamic distributed systems are proposed. From these studies, protocols (algorithms) will be developed to solve several basic problems of most distributed applications. These solutions will be aimed at dynamic environments. Therefore, the proposed protocols must have adaptability, foreseeing changes in the composition of the group of distributed computing participants. Furthermore, some infrastructures with important functionalities for security in distributed environments will be studied. These are the cases of an ACD (Distributed Certificate Authority) and an IDS (Distributed Intrusion Detection Systems). These infrastructures must make their functionalities available even in the presence of all the problems (failures, intrusions and churn) that involve dynamic distributed systems.
