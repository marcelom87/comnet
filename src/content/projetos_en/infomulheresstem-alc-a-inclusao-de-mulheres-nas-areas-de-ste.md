## Project Overview

This project aims to formalize, make compatible and strengthen a collaboration network between LAC countries to structure a regional observatory and carry out integrated extension and communication activities, contributing to the inclusion and retention of girls and women in the areas of Computing and ICTs. To operationalize these objectives, the project will articulate coordinated actions between partner institutions and organize its activities into three strategic axes:1. Actions and offer of activities to attract girls from basic education (primary and high school) to Computing and ICT courses;2. Proposing strategies for the retention of women in higher education courses, undergraduate and postgraduate, in Computing and ICTs; e3. Formulation of strategies to support women who work in the areas of Computing and ICT to advance and consolidate their careers, as professionals and managers, in academia, in the public and private professional sectors, as well as in entrepreneurship and innovation.
