## Project Overview

Fog Radio Access Networks (F- RANs) apply fog computing paradigms to cloud radio access networks. Artificial Intelligence (AI) techniques are useful to F-RANs in order to achieve enhanced energy efficiency, increased throughput, and decreased processing power consumption. In any scenario, the selection of the appropriate AI technique must consider the different time granularities at which decision-making occurs. This project investigates the benefits and challenges of implementing AI-driven F-RANs considering different time granularities: hours, minutes/seconds and milliseconds. Machine Learning (ML) techniques such as deep neural networks, reinforcement learning/online based algorithms and classifiers are investigated..
