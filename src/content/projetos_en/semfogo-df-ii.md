## Project Overview

The Secretariat of the Environment of the Federal District (Sema-DF), through Collaboration Agreement No. 01/2025, initiated the SEMFOGO-DF II Intelligent Monitoring of Fires in the Brasiliense Cerrado. The initiative, financed by the Single Environment Fund (Funam-DF), is carried out under a mutual cooperation regime between Sema and the GigaCandanga Association. The project aims to promote preventive and intelligent monitoring of forest fires, using artificial intelligence algorithms and computer vision for the early detection of fire and smoke outbreaks, ensuring quick and accurate responses from combat teams. SEMFOGO-DF II represents the expansion of the original project developed between 2022 and 2024 by the University of Brasília (UnB), in partnership with GigaCandanga and the Military Fire Department (CBMDF), with support from the Research Support Foundation (FAPDF). The initial phase allowed the creation of a functional prototype capable of recognizing images of fires and smoke through optical cameras installed in the Digital TV Tower, located in one of the highest areas of the DF. Based on this successful experience, SEMFOGO-DF II will expand the system's coverage with the installation of three new strategic surveillance points, ensuring continuous and real-time monitoring of large areas of Brasilia's territory. The new points cover the Brasília Botanical Garden, the Águas Amendmentadas Ecological Station and the JK Shopping tower, covering three other large and important regions of the Cerrado.
