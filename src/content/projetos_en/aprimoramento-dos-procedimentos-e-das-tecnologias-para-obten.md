## Project Overview

Is the interstate and international road passenger transport market (Triip) in Brazil regulated by the National Land Transport Agency? ANTT and operated, through grants, by private companies. In accordance with Law no. 8,987, of February 13, 1995, every concession or permission presupposes the provision of an adequate service, satisfying the conditions of regularity, continuity, efficiency, security, timeliness, generality and courtesy in its provision, and, to this end, it will be subject to inspection by the granting authority responsible for the delegation. With the aim of improving the quality of services provided by operating companies, ANTT has been improving its actions towards companies and, in this way, establishing greater control over the provision of services. At this point, one of the Agency's concerns is the reliability of the data made available by companies, as it uses them to disseminate statistics and carry out studies for the sector. In this sense, ANTT, in partnership with Ceftru, has been developing studies with the objective of evaluating the feasibility of implementing an automated operation data collection system and, with this, intends to obtain greater reliability in this data and, consequently, in the success of its actions. Regarding the studies developed, it can be said that this occurred in two distinct phases: in the first, the proposal for an automated model for data collection was developed; in the second, some tests were carried out to validate this model. This report refers to the second phase of the process, presenting all the stages related to the planning and development of the tests. In general terms, the present work aims to evaluate the proposed automated data collection model, in order to verify its feasibility of implementation within the system. The evaluation took place through two types of tests, in the field and in the laboratory.
