## Project Overview

The present research project aims to evaluate, propose and integrate different techniques used to guarantee the robustness of network systems. Our big challenge is the development of a unified framework that allows us to study the problem of robustness in network systems in a more generalized way. In the short term, we will advance the state of the art in this direction by taking advantage of the complementary skills of the researchers participating in this project. We will address specific problems, evaluating and integrating different techniques and theories, typically applied in the treatment of different problems. Ultimately, the objective is to establish and consolidate a collaborative and thematic research network involving a multi-institutional research group to investigate several highly challenging problems related to the robustness of network systems.
