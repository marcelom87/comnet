## Project Overview

This project aims to encourage and promote the training of professionals in the area of ​​offensive security in a practical way in the extreme south of Santa Catarina. To this end, competition team training will be organized, focused on cybersecurity competitions in the "Capture the Flag" (CTF) style. The target audience includes the general community, including professionals, enthusiasts and students from other institutions. In addition to training teams for competitions, the project seeks to stimulate the competitive offensive security scenario in Brazil, especially in the southern region. We count on the participation of UFSC students to create teaching materials and self-instructional courses, in addition to assembling and making available the software infrastructure necessary for CTF competitions. Lectures and practical demonstrations of cyber attacks will also be held, aiming to raise awareness about digital threats and attract new students to UFSC technology courses. The project has great potential for interaction with companies and government agencies, spreading knowledge in the area.
