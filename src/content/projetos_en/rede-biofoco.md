## Project Overview

The objective of this project is to create a bioinformatics research and development network integrating research and teaching institutions in the Midwest, capable of offering support to genomic and proteomic research groups (knowledge and tools), in addition to training and qualifying human resources in the areas of Biotechnology and Bioinfomatics in this region. The first and second versions of this project were both approved by CNPq, in 2003 and 2005, respectively. The third version was approved by FINEP in 2007.
