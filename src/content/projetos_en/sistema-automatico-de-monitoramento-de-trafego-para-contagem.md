## Project Overview

This project's general objective is to develop a portable system based on computer vision for counting the flow of vehicles, with their identification, on highways in order to enable cordon and line counting studies. SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES Automatic performance of the following tasks: 1. Monitoring with multiple cameras? Determination of the area, location and positioning of cameras? Acquisition of videos over long periods of time? Video processing by distributed computing 2. Video Processing? Vehicle detection? Rear location? Board segmentation? Character recognition 3. Data Processing ? Multimedia database development? Traffic database development? Data Mining 4. Preparation of Synthetic Source/Destination Matrices? Development of a procedure for obtaining O/D matrices? Development of a procedure for correcting O/D matrices? Development of a procedure for forecasting O/D matrices 5. Report Generation.
