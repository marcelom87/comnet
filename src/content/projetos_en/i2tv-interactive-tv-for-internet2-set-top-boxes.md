## Project Overview

Internet2 Infrastructure for Development and Testing of Programs and Tools for Interactive TV The I2TV project aims to support the formation of a consortium for the development and testing of applications and tools for Interactive TV in the Internet2 environment. The universities UFRN, PUC-Rio, USP-SP and UFSC and the television stations TVU-RN, TVE-RJ, TV Cultura-SP and TV Cultura-SC are part of the consortium. The project envisages the study of the three essential components in interactive broadband digital video applications: content generation, temporal displayers (formatters), present in set-top boxes, and distribution support systems (networks)...
