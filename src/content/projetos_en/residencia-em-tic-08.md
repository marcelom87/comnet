## Project Overview

Approved in Notice 01 - PPI - SOFTEX (8977084), MCTI Future Program, TPA N. 065/Softex/UnB Process MCTI 5.275/21. The ICT Residency Project 08 (Information Technology), an initiative of the Ministry of Science and Information Technology, developed with support from Softex (Organization linked to MCTI) trains professionals to meet the demand for 5G networks in the Federal District.
