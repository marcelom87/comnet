## Project Overview

This project aims to define and analyze the implementation of an analytical and experimental model in an environment that integrates several network technologies for the study of technologies and trends in the architecture of the Future Internet. So too, the implementation and development of advanced technologies for traffic engineering in wireless networks with multimedia applications with a special focus on mobile services, QoS and context-dependent applications, and QoS provisioning; study of QoS guarantee techniques in access networks in the presence of multimedia traffic as well as the proposition of methodologies for adaptive approaches in terms of planning, development, management and scalability; development of traffic models and optimization for multimedia traffic in wireless environments and development of protocols for adaptive routing, traffic aggregation when considering different control planes, cross-layer architectures, multicast routing, routing in multi-hop wireless networks and dynamic bandwidth allocation.
