## Project Overview

Due to the breadth of the research object, project management in the context of public organizations, which can only be achieved through the study of antecedent human (e.g., capabilities, motivations) and contextual (e.g., political-institutional, strategic, structural, procedural and technological environment) factors, the present Applied Research project has the general objective of carrying out studies on new artificial intelligence technologies, data science with a study of technological innovations, in the use of tools for automation and automation of FAP processes and Government of the Federal District GDF, as well as research and development of Business Intelligence BI tools, among other technologies, with an emphasis on system interoperability and corporate management of financial processes and budget management. The aforementioned object falls under art. 3 of Decree 10,426, of July 16, 2020. It fits into the interface between different areas of scientific knowledge: production engineering, mathematics, economics, computer science, administration, electrical engineering. For this reason, the group of researchers led by the proponent of this project is made up of professors from the University of Brasília linked to the Department of Electrical Engineering and other departments linked to the Faculty of Technology, who also work as researchers in the Postgraduate Programs of these same academic units. This group of professors and researchers has systematically contributed to the production of scientific knowledge and the development of management technologies in the areas of public sector economics, public finance and management of public organizations, being responsible for coordinating the Latitude Laboratory, the research group duly registered with the CNPq that brings together professors and students from doctoral, master's and undergraduate courses from various Faculties, Institutes and Departments of the University of Brasília.
