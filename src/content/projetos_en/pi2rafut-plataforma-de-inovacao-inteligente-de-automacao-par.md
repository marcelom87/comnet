## Project Overview

This proposal aims not only to contribute to the technological development of Brazil and the Federal District, but also to position itself at the forefront of mobile networks, especially in the context of Open RAN, an open and virtualized network architecture. With the aim of creating an environment conducive to the innovation and validation of next generation solutions, two main objectives are outlined: first, to consolidate an open and functional mobile network platform, which robustly integrates 5G and 6G technologies from different suppliers, allowing experimentation and validation of different use cases of the next generation of mobile networks; second, structure a software engineering environment aimed at developing intelligent applications, which integrates languages, hardware and artificial intelligence tools with the aim of optimizing mobile network resources. This proposed platform will not only enable the validation of solutions, from network software to optimization applications, but will also seek to train qualified professionals to work in the telecommunications market, meeting a demand identified by previous studies. Furthermore, international collaboration will be encouraged, strengthening partnerships with other research centers and promoting the exchange of knowledge and experiences. The outlined methodology involves not only the acquisition and configuration of equipment, but also the creation of a technological environment conducive to the continuous development of applications. This infrastructure, one of the most complete in Brazil, will include dedicated acceleration hardware for physical layer functions, allowing the execution of AI/ML algorithms and promoting innovations in the Brazilian telecommunications ecosystem. Finally, the proposal is not only aligned with ANATEL initiatives, but also seeks to boost the national and federal district markets by providing a platform for testing and validating Open RAN solutions, promoting significant advances in the country's telecommunications sector.
